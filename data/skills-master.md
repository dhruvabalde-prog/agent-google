## 1\. Capital Efficiency and Grants Scout

---

## name: capital-efficiency-and-grants-scout description: Identifies, navigates, and secures non-dilutive startup funding, government grants, state subsidies, and cloud credits across any region or industry. department: Sales, Business Development & Revenue Architecture allowed-tools: google context\_service\_agent drive

# Capital Efficiency and Grants Scout

**Department**: Sales, Business Development & Revenue Architecture

## Overview & Objective

Scouts and tracks non-dilutive capital opportunities—including government innovation grants, regional economic development subsidies, incubator stipends, and corporate cloud credit programs—customized to the venture's location and stage.

## Setup & Onboarding (Questions the Agent Will Ask)

To configure this skill, the Agent will present the following quick-select questions (MCQ or 1-2 word responses only):

1. **Operating Country** (1-2 words only): Country of operations (1-2 words): *(e.g., "India / United States / Singapore")*  
2. **State / Province** (1-2 words only): State, province, or region (1-2 words): *(e.g., "Haryana / California / Ontario")*  
3. **Venture Stage** \[MCQ\]: What is your venture's current stage?  
   - \[A\] Idea / Pre-Seed  
   - \[B\] Prototype / MVP Building  
   - \[C\] Early Revenue / Bootstrapped  
   - \[D\] Growth / Scaling  
4. **Target Cloud Credits** \[MCQ\]: Which cloud credit programs are you seeking?  
   - \[A\] Google Cloud for Startups  
   - \[B\] AWS Activate  
   - \[C\] Microsoft Founders Hub  
   - \[D\] All Major Cloud Providers

## Configuration Parameters (Fill-in-the-Blanks Schema)

| Parameter | Description | Valid Choices / Format | Default Fallback |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| `{{OPERATING_COUNTRY}}` | Country of legal incorporation or primary operations | India / United States / United Kingdom | User-provided country |
| `{{OPERATING_REGION_OR_STATE}}` | State, province, or economic zone of operations | Haryana / California / Ontario | User-provided state/province |
| `{{VENTURE_SECTOR}}` | Primary industry vertical and technology category | B2B SaaS / DeepTech / Clean Energy | Technology Startup |
| `{{TARGET_CREDIT_PROVIDERS}}` | Infrastructure providers sought for non-cash grants | GCP, AWS, Azure, OpenAI, Anthropic | AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft |
| `{{STARTUP_CERTIFICATION_STATUS}}` | Formal government recognition or incubator status | DPIIT Registered, YC W26, None | Unregistered / Early Stage |

## Operational Workflow

1. **Venture Profile Ingestion**: Load the user's `{{OPERATING_COUNTRY}}`, `{{OPERATING_REGION_OR_STATE}}`, and `{{VENTURE_SECTOR}}` parameters to filter applicable public and private funding streams.  
2. **Scheme & Grant Scanning**: Perform targeted web and database lookups for active grant application windows in `{{OPERATING_REGION_OR_STATE}}` and `{{OPERATING_COUNTRY}}` matching the venture's lifecycle stage.  
3. **Cloud & Infrastructure Stacking**: Check application criteria for `{{TARGET_CREDIT_PROVIDERS}}`, identifying prerequisite domain emails, website readiness, or incubator codes.  
4. **Eligibility & Compliance Audit**: Validate turnover caps, founder equity splits, and certification prerequisites against `{{STARTUP_CERTIFICATION_STATUS}}`.  
5. **Application Package Preparation**: Draft project pitch summaries, budget utilization schedules, and milestone roadmaps for grant submission.

## Guardrails & Gotchas

- **Entity Prerequisites**: Most regional subsidies require formal entity incorporation and bank account establishment before disbursement.  
- **Utilization Audits**: Non-dilutive grant funds almost universally require milestone-based expense reporting and formal utilization certification.  
- **Domain Email Mandate**: Cloud credit programs typically reject applications submitted from generic consumer email domains (@gmail.com).

# Needle Mover Strategy Evaluator

**Department**: Sales, Business Development & Revenue Architecture

## Overview & Objective

Assesses inbound opportunities, project proposals, and backlog tasks against strict 80/20 leverage metrics to eliminate low-yield cognitive traps.

## Setup & Onboarding (Questions the Agent Will Ask)

To configure this skill, the Agent will present the following quick-select questions (MCQ or 1-2 word responses only):

1. **Candidate Opportunity** (1-2 words only): Name of candidate initiative (1-2 words): *(e.g., "Client Pitch / Course Launch")*  
2. **Primary Value Metric** \[MCQ\]: What core outcome is this evaluating?  
   - \[A\] Direct Net Revenue  
   - \[B\] Strategic Distribution / Audience  
   - \[C\] High-Leverage Skill Acquisition  
   - \[D\] Operational Efficiency  
3. **Cognitive Drag Expectation** \[MCQ\]: Estimated ongoing operational burden:  
   - \[A\] Low (One-off or automated)  
   - \[B\] Medium (Requires weekly maintenance)  
   - \[C\] High (Demands major context switching)

## Configuration Parameters (Fill-in-the-Blanks Schema)

| Parameter | Description | Valid Choices / Format | Default Fallback |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| `{{OPPORTUNITY_NAME}}` | Name of the candidate project or opportunity | New Client Pitch / Side Product / Event Speaking | Candidate Initiative |
| `{{PRIMARY_ROI_METRIC}}` | The primary value metric being optimized | Net Revenue / Brand Authority / Strategic Learning | Core ROI Metric |
| `{{PARETO_BENCHMARK_PCT}}` | Threshold percentile defining high-leverage activities | Top 20% impact tier | Top 20% |

1. **Expected Upside Quantification**: Estimate the potential impact of `{{OPPORTUNITY_NAME}}` against `{{PRIMARY_ROI_METRIC}}`.  
2. **Cognitive Friction & Overhead Calculation**: Quantify recurring maintenance costs, context-switching drag, and dependencies.  
3. **Pareto Scoring**: Benchmark the opportunity against existing top-tier initiatives in `{{PARETO_BENCHMARK_PCT}}`.  
4. **Action Recommendation**: Issue an authoritative Pursue, Delegate, Automate, or Eliminate verdict with supporting rationale.

## Guardrails & Gotchas

- **Productive Procrastination Trap**: Beware of tasks that feel like work (e.g., logo design, endless research) but yield zero actual business progress.  
- **Saying No to Good Ideas**: High-yield execution requires ruthlessly turning down good opportunities to protect space for great ones.

   - \[B\] Pro / Professional (\$49 \- \$99/mo)
